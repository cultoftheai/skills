# Auto mode

- **Step 5.** Do not ask. Use your triage as it is.
- **No declines.** A thread that you would decline becomes needs-you. Its reply gives your reason to decline and names the decision that a person must make.
- **Needs-you.** Do not change code for a needs-you thread. Leave it open for a person.
- **Assumptions.** In `REVIEW.md`, under `## Assumptions`, write that no person confirmed the triage. Add one line for each thread that you changed from decline to needs-you.

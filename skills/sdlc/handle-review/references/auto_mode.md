# Auto mode

- **Step 4.** Do not ask. Use your triage as it is.
- **No declines.** A thread that you would decline becomes `needs-you`. Its reply gives your reason to decline and names the decision that a person must make.
- **Needs-you.** Do not change code or spec files for a `needs-you` thread. Leave it open for a person.
- **Assumptions.** In `<work.path>/<id>/review-triage.md` (never committed), under `## Assumptions`, write that no person confirmed the triage. Add one line for each thread that you changed from `decline` to `needs-you`.
